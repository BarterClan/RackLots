Common stationery & packaging surplus scenarios

From FMCG packaging redesigns to government tender overruns — we match every type of surplus with the right buyer.

🏭

FMCG packaging redesigns

New brand identity or regulatory label changes create large volumes of pre-printed packaging that must clear fast.

🏢

Corporate office closures

Companies downsizing often have years of stationery stock — bulk buyers preferred for quick clearance.

🖨️

Printing press overruns

Print overruns, cancelled client jobs, season-specific packaging with limited shelf life — needs fast movers.

📋

Government tender surplus

Post-tender procurement overruns from government departments and PSUs — often clean, unused stock in bulk.

📦

E-commerce packaging excess

E-commerce operations winding down — custom-printed boxes, void fill, tape, and branded packaging to clear.

🔄

SKU rationalisation

FMCG and retail companies rationalising SKUs leave behind product-specific labels, inserts, and packaging in volume.
