Our story

Why we built racklots.com

It started with a conversation. A procurement director at an electronics company in Delhi told us about a batch of 4,000 units — last year's smartphone model. His broker offered ₹180 per unit. His landed cost was ₹820 per unit. He had no real alternative. He took the deal.

That was a ₹25 lakh loss. On one SKU. In one quarter. And it happened every season.

"We just deal with it because there's no better option." — Procurement Director, Delhi electronics company

We heard versions of this story from almost every procurement manager we spoke to across India. The broker model is built on information asymmetry — brokers know what your inventory is worth on the open market, and you don't. That gap is their profit margin, at your expense.

We built racklots.com to change that. A marketplace where buyers compete for your inventory. Where you see real market pricing in real time. Where deals close in 48 hours. Where every transaction is GST-compliant and fully documented.

No brokers. No opacity. No weeks of waiting. Just direct connections between verified Indian businesses.

Our mission

A fair market for every lot of inventory

India generates over ₹80,000 crore in surplus inventory every year. Most of it is liquidated through broker channels at 20–25% of value — leaving hundreds of crores on the table for manufacturers, distributors, and retailers across every category.

Our mission is to build the infrastructure that makes surplus inventory trading transparent, fast, and fair for every business in India — from a small distributor in Surat to a large manufacturer in Pune.

Radical transparency

Every buyer sees the same listing. Every seller sees every offer. No hidden deals, no information games.

Verified trust

Every business on our platform is KYB-verified. You know exactly who you're dealing with before any money changes hands.

Speed as a feature

Surplus inventory depreciates every day it sits. We've built everything around closing deals in 48 hours — not 48 days.

Better recovery for sellers

When buyers compete, sellers win. Our verified buyer network consistently delivers 40% better recovery than broker channels.

Full GST compliance

Every transaction on our platform generates compliant documentation. No informal deals, no compliance risk.

Built for India

Designed specifically for the Indian B2B market — its payment systems, logistics realities, regulatory requirements, and business culture.
