Buyer FAQs

Common questions from buyers

How do I know sellers are legitimate?
Every seller on racklots.com goes through our KYB (Know Your Business) verification process before they can list inventory. We verify GST registration, business registration, and identity documents. You can also see a seller's transaction history and ratings.
What is the minimum order quantity?
MOQ varies by listing and is set by the seller. Some listings have no minimum — you can buy the full lot or part of it. Others require a minimum order, which is clearly stated on each listing. You can filter by MOQ when browsing.
How does payment and escrow work?
Once a deal is agreed, you deposit funds into our secure escrow account. The seller is notified and arranges dispatch. Funds are released to the seller only after you confirm receipt of inventory in the agreed condition. This protects both parties.
Can I inspect inventory before buying?
Yes — for large deals, you can request a physical inspection or sample before committing. For smaller deals, sellers provide detailed photos, condition grading, and documentation. Our deal support team can help coordinate inspections.
Will I get a GST invoice?
Yes, absolutely. Every transaction on racklots.com generates a GST-compliant tax invoice from the seller. You can claim full input tax credit on all purchases. We also provide transaction records for your accounting team.
Who arranges logistics and delivery?
Logistics terms are negotiated between buyer and seller on each deal. Some sellers offer delivery to your warehouse; others require buyer pickup. Our platform clearly shows logistics details on each listing so you can factor it into your offer.
